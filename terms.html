1. What Brown Shop is

Brown Shop is a marketplace where students at Brown University and the Rhode Island School of

Design buy and sell secondhand items from each other and hand them off in person on campus.

We are a venue, not a seller. Every listing is created by a student. When you buy something,

your contract is with the seller, not with us. We do not own, inspect, store, ship, or guarantee

any item.

**We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Brown University or the Rhode Island

School of Design.** School names, email domains, and the campus locations we list are used only to

identify the community the app serves.

2. Who can use it

You may use Brown Shop if all of these are true:

  • You are 17 or older.
  • You have a working @brown.edu or @risd.edu email address, and it is yours.
  • You have not previously been removed from Brown Shop.
  • You can form a binding contract where you live.

We verify your school by the domain of the email address you sign up with and by requiring you to

click a link we send to it. We do not otherwise confirm enrollment. If you stop being able to

receive email at that address, you may lose access to your account.

One account per person. Do not share your login, and tell us at asafgreenfield21+brownshop@gmail.com if you think

someone else has it.

5. Buying and selling

Prices are set by sellers. Buyers can send offers; sellers can accept, decline, or counter

once. Offers expire 48 hours after they are made or countered.

Payment happens in the app. We use Stripe to process card payments and

to pay sellers. We never see or store your full card number. Sellers must complete Stripe's

onboarding (including identity verification) before they can be paid.

We hold the money until the handoff. When you buy, your card is charged and the funds are held.

The seller offers meetup times; you pick one; you meet on campus and exchange the item. **The

money is released to the seller when the buyer confirms the exchange in the app.** A seller

confirming alone never completes an order.

Fees. Brown Shop keeps a platform fee of 3.5% of the item price plus $0.45 on each

completed sale. The buyer pays the listed price; the fee comes out of the seller's payout, and both

numbers are shown before you commit. Fees may change with notice for future orders.

Cancellations and refunds. Either side can cancel before the handoff is confirmed; the buyer is

refunded in full to the original payment method and the listing goes back up for sale. Refunds take

as long as the card network takes, usually 5–10 business days. If a meetup never happens, cancel the

order — do not just stop responding.

Disputes. We are not a court and we do not arbitrate who is right about an item's condition.

We will look at reports, and we can cancel and refund an order, remove a listing, or suspend an

account. Chargebacks initiated with your bank may result in your account being suspended while

they are resolved.

Taxes. Selling your own used belongings for less than you paid is usually not taxable income,

but you are responsible for your own taxes. If you sell enough that Stripe must file a form for you,

Stripe will collect what it needs directly.

6. Meeting in person — read this part

Every Brown Shop transaction ends with two people meeting. **We do not vet, background-check, or

supervise anyone.** Verifying a school email is not a safety guarantee.

  • Meet at the public, curated campus spots the app offers. Do not meet in a dorm room or a car.
  • Meet during the day when you can, and tell a friend where you are going.
  • Inspect the item before you confirm the exchange in the app. Confirming releases the money.
  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, leave. Then block and report them in the app.
  • If you feel unsafe, call Brown DPS at 401-863-4111 or 911. Report it to us afterwards.

You meet at your own risk, and you accept that risk when you use Brown Shop.

7. Reporting and blocking

Every profile, listing, message thread, and order has a Report action, and you can block

anyone. Blocking stops them from messaging you or making offers, and hides their listings from you.

Reports are private — the person you report is not told who reported them. We review reports and

act at our discretion; we are not obliged to tell you the outcome.

8. Suspension and termination

to, or if we reasonably believe you are putting other people at risk. You can stop using Brown Shop

whenever you want, and you can delete your account in Settings → Delete account (see §11).

Open orders survive termination: money already held is still refunded or released according to §5.
